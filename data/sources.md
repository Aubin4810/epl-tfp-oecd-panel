# Data Sources

### 1. Penn World Tables (PWT 10.0)
**Variables:** TFP, Human Capital
**Link:** https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/pwt-releases/pwt100

### 2. OECD EPL Index
**Variables:** Employment Protection Legislation index
**Link:** https://www.oecd.org/employment/emp/oecdindicatorsofemploymentprotection.htm

### 3. OECD Economic Outlook — Output Gap
**Variables:** Output gap
**Link:** https://stats.oecd.org/ → Economic Outlook → Output gap

### 4. OECD Tax-Benefit Database — Net Replacement Rate
**Variables:** Net replacement rate
**Link:** https://www.oecd.org/social/benefits-and-wages/

### 5. OECD — ALMP Spending
**Variables:** Public spending on active labour market policies (% GDP)
**Link:** https://stats.oecd.org/ → Social Protection → Public spending on ALMP

### 6. EU KLEMS
**Variables:** Labour productivity growth
**Link:** https://euklems-intanprod-llee.luiss.it/

### 7. World Bank — WDI
**Variables:** Trade, Unemployment, Inflation, GDP per capita, GFCF, FDI, Population growth, Government consumption
**Link:** https://databank.worldbank.org/source/world-development-indicators
